Consent to Processing of Personal and Health Data

Last updated: 21 November 2026

By creating an account or using the features of the FITA app, I freely and of my own will give FITA my consent to process my personal data, including data about my health, on the terms below. I confirm that I am at least 16 years old.

1. Data covered

I consent to the processing of the following data:

  • name, email address and account data;
  • phone number, if I choose to provide it;
  • date of birth and sex;
  • height, weight and other body measurements;
  • training, physical activity and progress data, and my goals;
  • health data that I allow FITA to read from Apple Health or Health Connect, such as sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, steps and workouts;
  • my conversations with the FITA coach;
  • any other data I provide in the app voluntarily.

2. Purposes

My data is processed in order to:

  • register and identify me;
  • give me access to the features of the app;
  • keep my account and sync my data between devices;
  • show me my own health data;
  • generate training recommendations, insights and plans, including with the help of AI;
  • personalise content and the interface;
  • provide technical support and send service notifications;
  • improve the quality of the Service and develop the product;
  • comply with legal obligations.

My health data is used only to show me my data and to power the coach. It is not used for advertising or marketing and is not sold.

3. Operations on the data

I consent to the following operations on my data: collection, recording, organisation, storage, adaptation, use, transfer to the service providers described below, anonymisation, restriction, erasure and destruction, as well as other operations needed for the purposes above.

4. Service providers and the AI coach

I agree that FITA may engage providers that process my data on its behalf: server hosting (DigitalOcean, data centre in Amsterdam, the Netherlands, EU), email delivery (Resend, EU region), push notifications and self-hosted analytics.

To generate the coach’s answers, my message and the context needed for it (relevant health and training data, profile details and the coach’s notes) are sent to a third-party AI provider. [AI provider: to be confirmed.]

Data is transferred only for these purposes and only to the extent the Service needs. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

5. MCP connector

The FITA connector (mcp.fita.fit), which gives an AI assistant of my choice access to my FITA data, is not covered by this consent by default. It works only if I separately enable it and authorise a specific assistant in the app, and I can disconnect it at any time.

6. Duration

This consent is valid until the purposes of processing are achieved, until I withdraw it or until I delete my account, whichever comes first.

7. Withdrawing consent

I can withdraw this consent at any time in the app settings or by writing to support@appfita.ru. After that, FITA stops processing my data unless it has another legal basis to continue. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before it. I can also revoke FITA’s access to health data at any time in the settings of Apple Health or Health Connect.

8. Confirmation

By tapping the sign-up button or ticking the consent checkbox, I confirm that I have read the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use and that I give my consent to the processing of my personal and health data on the terms above.
