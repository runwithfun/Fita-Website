Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FITA (“FITA”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, stores and protects the personal data of people who use the FITA apps and the fita.fit website (together, the “Service”), and what rights you have over that data.

FITA is a health app used around the world. We apply the same standard to everyone, based on the principles of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR): we collect only what the Service needs, use it only for the purposes described here, and give you control over it. FITA is not a medical service.

1. Who we are

FITA is the controller of the personal data described in this Policy. For any privacy question or request, write to support@appfita.ru.

User
a person who uses the Service.
Personal data
any information relating to an identified or identifiable person.
Processing
any operation on personal data, such as collection, recording, organisation, storage, adaptation, use, disclosure, anonymisation, restriction, erasure or destruction.

2. Data we process

  • Account data: email address, name, username and account identifiers.
  • Profile data you enter: date of birth, sex, height, weight and other body measurements, goals, training plans and preferences, how you feel and how you recover.
  • Health and fitness data from Apple Health or Health Connect, only with your permission: for example sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, steps, activity, workouts and other metrics recorded by your phone or by devices you have connected to those platforms (see section 3).
  • Coach conversations: the messages you send to the FITA coach, its replies and the notes it keeps to remember context.
  • Support messages you send us.
  • Technical data: IP address and the approximate country derived from it, device model, operating system version, app version, interface language, app and installation identifiers, crash reports, in-app events and server logs.
  • Website data: pages visited and how you arrived at the site (see section 12).

If you choose to share additional information in forms, in the coach chat or in support requests, we process it as well. Please do not share information you do not want processed as part of the Service.

3. Health data

FITA reads data from Apple Health (iOS) or Health Connect (Android) only after you grant permission in the system dialog. You can change or withdraw that permission at any time in the settings of Apple Health or Health Connect.

We use health data only to show you your own data and to power the FITA coach — the insights, recommendations and plans it gives you. We do not use health data for advertising or marketing, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with data brokers or other third parties, except the service providers listed in section 7 that process it on our behalf to run these features.

Health data is kept mainly on your device. When you use the coach, the context needed for an answer is sent to our servers and to the AI provider. Compact summaries (for example daily totals, sleep and workouts) may be stored on our servers to sync your data between devices and, if you turn it on, to serve the MCP connector (section 6).

We process health data on the basis of your explicit consent. You can withdraw it at any time (section 10).

4. Purposes and legal bases

  • creating and running your account, signing you in, syncing your data and providing the features of the Service — performance of our contract with you;
  • processing health data, personalised recommendations and the AI coach — your explicit consent;
  • technical support and service notifications — performance of the contract;
  • security, preventing fraud and abuse, diagnosing errors — our legitimate interests in keeping the Service safe and working;
  • improving the Service, the user experience and our algorithms, using our own self-hosted analytics — our legitimate interests;
  • marketing and newsletters — only with your consent;
  • complying with legal obligations — legal obligation.

5. The AI coach

The FITA coach uses a third-party AI model. To generate an answer, we send the AI provider your message and the context needed for it: relevant health and training data, profile details and the coach’s notes. [AI provider: to be confirmed.]

The provider processes this data on our behalf only to generate the answer. We do not use your health data to train AI models. Coach answers are general information, not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

6. The MCP connector

The FITA connector (mcp.fita.fit) lets you give an AI assistant of your choice access to your FITA data. It is off by default and works only after you explicitly enable it and authorise a specific assistant.

Once connected, that assistant can read the FITA data available through the connector to answer your questions. The assistant’s provider processes this data under its own privacy policy. You can disconnect an assistant at any time in the app; its access ends immediately.

7. Service providers

We use a small number of providers that process data on our behalf, under contract, only as needed to run the Service:

  • DigitalOcean — servers and databases, in a data centre in Amsterdam, the Netherlands (EU);
  • Resend — sign-in codes and service emails, EU region;
  • OpenPanel — product analytics, self-hosted on our own servers; no analytics data goes to a third-party analytics company;
  • AI provider for the coach — [AI provider: to be confirmed];
  • Apple and Google push notification services, if you enable notifications, and their app stores, which distribute the app under their own policies.

We determine your approximate country from your IP address using the IP to Country Lite database by DB-IP.

We may disclose data if the law requires it or to protect the rights and safety of our users and the Service. We do not sell personal data.

8. Where data is stored

Our servers and databases are located in the European Union (Amsterdam, the Netherlands). If a provider processes data outside the European Economic Area, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. How long we keep data

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes in this Policy. Account, profile and health data are kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your data from our servers; copies in backups are removed as backups rotate. Server logs are kept for as long as needed to diagnose errors and protect the Service. We may keep some data longer where the law requires it.

10. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can:

  • access your personal data and get a copy of it;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • delete your data and your account;
  • restrict processing;
  • port your data — receive it in a structured, machine-readable format;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and to marketing at any time;
  • withdraw consent at any time in the app settings or by email; this does not affect processing that took place before;
  • complain to a data protection authority in your country.

To use these rights, write to support@appfita.ru. We answer within one month. FITA does not make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects based solely on automated processing.

11. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time in the app, under Profile. Data in Apple Health or Health Connect stays there under your control: FITA only reads it.

12. Website, cookies and browser storage

The fita.fit website does not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers. It uses your browser’s local storage to keep an anonymous visit identifier and to remember how you arrived at the site (for example, from which campaign or invitation link). This data goes only to our own analytics. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

13. Security

We protect personal data with technical and organisational measures: data is encrypted in transit, access to servers and databases is limited to the people who need it, and we monitor the Service for errors and abuse. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your data from unauthorised or accidental access, alteration, disclosure or loss.

14. Children

The Service is not intended for children under 16. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

15. Marketing messages

We send news and promotional messages only with your consent. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in the email, in the app settings or by writing to us.

16. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy. The current version is always published on this page with its date at the top. If we make material changes, we will tell you in the app or by email before they take effect.
